About

This page is a personal research notebook kept by Minjae Lee, a PhD student in Operations Management/Management Science at the University of Maryland's Robert H. Smith School of Business.

What it is

Each morning, an automated pipeline collects news on topics that I'm currently interested in, including sustainable operations, ESG, supply chain resilience, energy and AI data centers, the circular economy and related topics. It picks a handful of stories and adds an operations-research angle to each: why it matters, a possible research question, data that could be used to study it, and related academic papers.

Why it exists

To follow real-world developments that could motivate empirical or analytical research, and to build a searchable archive of ideas, story threads and data leads over time.

How it is made

  • Stories come from public news feeds (specialist trade publications and Google News); academic papers come from Crossref and OpenAlex.
  • Story selection and summaries are generated automatically with Claude (Anthropic) and are not reviewed one by one before they appear here. They can contain mistakes. Please check the linked original sources before citing or relying on anything on this site.
  • Research questions and data suggestions are brainstorming prompts, not findings.

Non-commercial

This is a non-commercial project for personal research and learning: no ads, no sponsorship, no paid content. Headlines and articles belong to their original publishers; summaries are kept short and always link to the source. Views here are my own and do not represent the University of Maryland.

Privacy
